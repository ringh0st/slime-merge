Slime Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Slime Merge does not collect, store or share any personal data.

The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't send your data to any server. Your best score, the slimes you've discovered and your sound setting are saved only on your own device, and deleting the app removes them.

The app makes no network requests while you play. If you play in a web browser, the website host (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical data such as IP addresses, as with any website.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/ringh0st/slime-merge.
